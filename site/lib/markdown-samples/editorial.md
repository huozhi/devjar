# The last train

By the time the platform emptied, the sea had turned the color of an old photograph.

## Between one place and another

The station clock was slow. Nobody seemed to mind. A woman folded her newspaper, and somewhere behind the ticket office a kettle began to boil.

> A journey sometimes begins with the decision to wait.

I left the timetable in my pocket and watched the light move across the rails.
