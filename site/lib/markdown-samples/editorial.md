# The Garden After Rain

The gate was still wet when she opened it. Beyond the path, every leaf held a little of the morning, and the garden seemed to have moved closer during the night.

## A quieter way of looking

She had come to count the new flowers. Instead, she stopped beside the stone wall and watched a drop gather at the tip of a fern.

> There are days when attention is the only thing we need to bring with us.

Nothing remarkable happened. The drop fell, another began to form, and a bird somewhere beyond the wall tried the same brief phrase again.

## What remains

By noon the stones would be dry. For now, she left the notebook closed and followed the path to its end.
