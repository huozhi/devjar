# The last train

[The platform](./index.md) · [The wait](./waiting.md) · [Morning](./morning.md)

By the time the platform emptied, the sea had turned the color of an old photograph.

The station clock was slow. Nobody seemed to mind. A woman folded her newspaper; behind the ticket office, a kettle began to boil.

> A journey sometimes begins with the decision to wait.

I left the timetable in my pocket.
