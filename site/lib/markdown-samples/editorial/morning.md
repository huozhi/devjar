# The first departure

[The platform](./index.md) · [The wait](./waiting.md) · [Morning](./morning.md)

In the morning the platform looked smaller. Someone had swept the leaves into a careful pile beside the steps.

The train arrived without an announcement.

> The sea was still there. I had simply stopped checking.

I took a seat by the window and left the notebook open.
