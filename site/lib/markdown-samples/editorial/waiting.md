# An hour unclaimed

[The platform](./index.md) · [The wait](./waiting.md) · [Morning](./morning.md)

The café had three tables and no menu. I asked for tea. The owner brought bread as well, without making a ceremony of it.

Outside, the tracks held a thin line of light.

## Nothing to hurry

I wrote down the names of the stations I would not reach that evening. Then I turned the page and drew the cup.

It was the first useful thing I had done all day.
