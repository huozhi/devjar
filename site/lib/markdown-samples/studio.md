# A Little More Focus

Good tools leave space for your ideas. This week, we made a few small changes to help a working session feel less interrupted.

## Pick up where you left off

Your last document is ready when you return. The page remembers its place, so the next thought can follow the previous one.

> Keep the essential action close. Let everything else wait.

## Small details, less friction

- A quieter workspace with fewer competing controls.
- Clearer names for the things you use every day.
- A faster path from an unfinished note to a shared idea.

## What comes next

We are trying these changes with a small group of readers. Their habits will tell us which details deserve another pass.
